# Your first real listing — step by step

Labels in «» are the Greek buttons/sections you will see in the app.

**Before you start:** have the property facts at hand (area from the title deed or contract, floor, year of construction, heating,
energy certificate class if issued, monthly common charges), 10–30 good photos as JPEG (on iPhone: Settings → Camera → Formats →
"Most Compatible", or export HEIC photos as JPEG), and the floor plan as an image if you have one.

1. **Start the app** (`npm run dev`) and open `http://localhost:3000`. Click «Δημιουργία αγγελίας» → create your account
   (choose «Ιδιοκτήτης ή μεσίτης» or «Μεσιτικό γραφείο»).
2. In «Ρυθμίσεις», fill in the display name and contact details (and agency logo, if any). New listings copy these as defaults.
3. On «Αγγελίες» click «+ Νέα αγγελία».
4. **Βήμα 1 — Βασικά στοιχεία**: purpose, type, region/municipality/neighbourhood, price (or «Τιμή κατόπιν επικοινωνίας»), area,
   bedrooms, bathrooms, floor, availability. Choose how precise the public location is; the street address stays private unless you
   pick «Πλήρης διεύθυνση». Click «Αποθήκευση & συνέχεια».
5. **Βήμα 2 — Χαρακτηριστικά**: answer what you know; use «Δεν είναι γνωστό» otherwise. Describe real limitations in «Περιορισμοί ή
   γνωστά ζητήματα» — they will be stated plainly.
6. **Βήμα 3 — Φωτογραφίες**: drag all photos in. Put them in the order a visitor would walk through the home, assign each to a room
   (use «Ονομασία» for "Υπνοδωμάτιο 2" etc.), choose the cover, untick photos you don't want published. Optional: «Εκδόσεις» → under
   «Βασική διόρθωση (ενσωματωμένη)» click «Δημιουργία βελτιωμένης έκδοσης» and compare with the original before choosing «Χρήση αυτής της έκδοσης».
7. **Βήμα 4 — Κάτοψη** (optional): upload the plan, pick a room, click on the plan where it is.
8. **Βήμα 5 — Πληρότητα**: follow the suggestions until all core items are complete.
9. **Βήμα 6 — Κείμενα**: write (or generate, if AI is configured) the Greek title and description, edit them, then
   «Αποθήκευση & έγκριση» on each. Resolve any yellow «Ελέγξτε αυτές τις φράσεις» warnings. Add English if you want international
   visitors.
10. Open «Προεπισκόπηση» and read the page as a buyer would — on your phone too.
11. **Βήμα 7 — Έλεγχος & έγκριση**: confirm contact options (phone/email visibility, enquiry form), add a Matterport/YouTube tour link
    if you have one, tick the confirmation and click «Έγκριση αγγελίας».
12. **Βήμα 8 — Δημοσίευση & αρχεία**: choose «Μόνο με σύνδεσμο» for a first test and click «Δημοσίευση αγγελίας». Open the link in a
    private browser window, send yourself a test enquiry, and check «Μηνύματα».
13. Download the PDF brochure, factsheet and full package; check the Greek text, photos and contact details.
14. Change something (e.g. price): the live page keeps the old version until you approve again and click «Ενημέρωση δημοσιευμένης
    σελίδας». Try «Απόσυρση αγγελίας» to confirm the page disappears.

What to note while testing your real property: anything confusing in the questionnaire, missing options for your property type,
photo order/rotation problems, PDF layout with your real photos, how long uploads take on your connection, and wording you would
change in the Greek UI.
